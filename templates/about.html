🔍 About Deepfake Investigation System

Next-generation artificial intelligence forensics platform for image & video authenticity verification.

🛡️ Mission & Overview

The Deepfake Investigation System is an enterprise-grade digital forensic tool designed to detect sophisticated facial manipulation, generative AI face swaps, and synthetic media artifacts in images and videos. Utilizing state-of-the-art EfficientNet deep convolutional neural networks combined with Explainable AI (Grad-CAM), the platform delivers verified authenticity scores, risk evaluations, and printable forensic evidence reports.

⚙️ Core Technology Stack

🧠 EfficientNet-B3 CNN
🔥 Grad-CAM Attention Maps
👁️ OpenCV Face Detection
⚡ TensorFlow 2.x Engine
🌐 Flask Web Framework
🗄️ SQLite Forensic Registry
📄 ReportLab PDF Engine
